# IT Support Handbook

## Factory Reset With Data Wipe

To factory-reset the company laptop and permanently wipe all local documents and settings, open Settings, choose Recovery, select Reset this PC and pick Remove everything. Use this procedure only when the laptop is being reassigned or decommissioned.

## Factory Reset Preserving Documents

To factory-reset the company laptop safely while preserving your local documents, open Settings, choose Recovery, select Reset this PC and pick Keep my files. Installed applications are removed, but files in your user folders are kept. Back up anything outside your user folders first.

## Upload Error Codes

Error code E-4012 means the upload quota for your account was exceeded. Delete old files or request a quota increase from the service desk. Error code E-4031 means the file type is blocked by policy; executable files and disk images cannot be uploaded.

## VPN Access

Connect to the corporate VPN before accessing internal dashboards. If the VPN client reports an authentication failure, re-enrol your hardware security key through the self-service portal.
